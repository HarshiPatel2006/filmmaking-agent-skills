# Dialogue and subtext

## Inputs and deliverable

- Accept: character goals; relationship; necessary information; scene beats.
- Produce: dialogue draft, surface-versus-intent notes, revision suggestions.
- If critical inputs are missing, state assumptions, distinguish planning from observed footage, and mark any untestable decision for review. Do not invent measurements, assets or runtime results.

## Decision procedure

1. Identify the speaker's tactical objective and the listener's likely resistance before writing a line.
2. Give each character distinctive syntax, rhythm, vocabulary and avoidance patterns grounded in their history, not demographic stereotypes.
3. Separate surface utterance from underlying intent; use pauses, actions and reactions to carry what cannot be said directly.
4. Move necessary information into motivated exchanges or visual evidence; avoid characters telling each other what both know.
5. Read aloud; remove repetition and generic emotional declarations, then check that the listener's response is plausible.
6. Allow direct speech when candor is the dramatic event; subtext is a choice, not a universal requirement.

## Diagnose and correct

Do not make every line cryptic. If neither action nor words communicates the objective, add a clear anchor.

## Output checks

- Deliver the specified artifact, with decisions tied to identifiable scenes, beats or shots when relevant.
- Show evidence or reasoning for the selected approach and at least one plausible alternative when the decision is consequential; state whether it is planned or actually observed.
- Verify the specific failure mode above is not present, or document the creative reason and the test that would demonstrate success.
- Use the original practice case below to check that the procedure produces a concrete, reviewable result rather than a definition or generic advice.

## Practice case

A character asks whether the kettle is still on while actually testing if their partner is leaving; write the exchange and identify the decisive observable beat.
