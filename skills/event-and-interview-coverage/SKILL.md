# Event and interview coverage

## Inputs and deliverable

- Accept: event schedule; access; subjects; audio needs; consent.
- Produce: coverage plan, priority moments, camera/audio fallback and edit options.
- If critical inputs are missing, state assumptions, distinguish planning from observed footage, and mark any untestable decision for review. Do not invent measurements, assets or runtime results.

## Decision procedure

1. Identify nonrepeatable moments and information the viewer needs; plan an uninterrupted wide or safety view where feasible.
2. For interviews, choose intentional gaze and background, clean sound and meaningful cutaways; avoid staging reactions falsely.
3. For live performances, plan angle variety and sync; prioritize continuity of the actual event over arbitrary camera movement.
4. For music videos, distinguish narrative visuals from visible performance; when lip-sync is intended, use an approved timing reference and verify alignment.
5. Plan discreet operation, permissions and backup coverage; preserve chronological accuracy or clearly label reconstructed footage.

## Diagnose and correct

Do not combine footage from different performances as if factually identical when timing, audience or actions differ. Do not use an unrelated cutaway to misrepresent a speaker.

## Output checks

- Deliver the specified artifact, with decisions tied to identifiable scenes, beats or shots when relevant.
- Show evidence or reasoning for the selected approach and at least one plausible alternative when the decision is consequential; state whether it is planned or actually observed.
- Verify the specific failure mode above is not present, or document the creative reason and the test that would demonstrate success.
- Use the original practice case below to check that the procedure produces a concrete, reviewable result rather than a definition or generic advice.

## Practice case

A one-time recital includes a solo and audience reaction; ensure continuous audio and a safety shot with relevant alternative angles.
