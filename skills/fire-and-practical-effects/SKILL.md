# Fire and practical effects

## Inputs and deliverable

- Accept: visible source; scene duration; subject positions; intended effect.
- Produce: effect timing and source map, reflection/shadow cues, safe execution constraint.
- If critical inputs are missing, state assumptions, distinguish planning from observed footage, and mark any untestable decision for review. Do not invent measurements, assets or runtime results.

## Decision procedure

1. Identify the apparent source location, size, direction, relative brightness and expected temporal pattern.
2. For flames, use subtle irregular variation and warm local bounce only where physically or expressively appropriate; avoid synchronized artificial pulsing of the entire scene.
3. For lightning or passing lamps, coordinate direction, intensity and timing across faces, background, shadows and reflections.
4. Keep essential expressions readable; match effects across reverse coverage and consecutive clips.
5. Specify safe modern lighting or simulated effects; actual fire, high voltage, hazardous rigs and weather effects require qualified professionals.

## Diagnose and correct

Avoid copying legacy methods involving exposed arcs, burning materials or dangerous electrical setups. A moving light effect needs consistent spatial response.

## Output checks

- Deliver the specified artifact, with decisions tied to identifiable scenes, beats or shots when relevant.
- Show evidence or reasoning for the selected approach and at least one plausible alternative when the decision is consequential; state whether it is planned or actually observed.
- Verify the specific failure mode above is not present, or document the creative reason and the test that would demonstrate success.
- Use the original practice case below to check that the procedure produces a concrete, reviewable result rather than a definition or generic advice.

## Practice case

A candlelit conversation briefly interrupted by a flash outside: maintain candle direction and coordinate the separate flash in both angles.
