# Character arc

## Inputs and deliverable

- Accept: character profile; causal outline; intended ending.
- Produce: arc ledger with belief, choice, cost, consequence and evidence per turning point.
- If critical inputs are missing, state assumptions, distinguish planning from observed footage, and mark any untestable decision for review. Do not invent measurements, assets or runtime results.

## Decision procedure

1. Set baseline belief and habitual tactic; specify what the story tests.
2. At each major pressure point, show a choice and its consequence rather than simply labeling a new emotion.
3. Determine whether the character changes, refuses change, deteriorates or reveals a stable value; do not force positive growth.
4. Make the final choice echo or contrast with an early choice, and show observable evidence of changed or persistent behavior.
5. Check intermediate beats for earned progression and relationships for reciprocal change.

## Diagnose and correct

Avoid an instantaneous conversion without pressure or evidence; add a preceding failure, insight or cost.

## Output checks

- Deliver the specified artifact, with decisions tied to identifiable scenes, beats or shots when relevant.
- Show evidence or reasoning for the selected approach and at least one plausible alternative when the decision is consequential; state whether it is planned or actually observed.
- Verify the specific failure mode above is not present, or document the creative reason and the test that would demonstrate success.
- Use the original practice case below to check that the procedure produces a concrete, reviewable result rather than a definition or generic advice.

## Practice case

A sibling who avoids responsibility later stays to help: require a sequence of increasingly costly decisions, not an unmotivated final speech.
