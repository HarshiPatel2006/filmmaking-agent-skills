# Research gaps and limits

- No claim of exhaustive human page-by-page reading. The four text-bearing PDFs were fully extracted and indexed; selected passages and chapter ranges were reviewed. The Mascelli scan was sampled visually across all five chapters (including diagrams and later sections), not fully transcribed. Read the remaining passages and plate captions before calling coverage exhaustive.
- Advanced directing of actors, consent/intimacy coordination, costume fabrication, makeup, production management, set construction and specialized safety require additional professional sources. The design skills here are primarily cross-department brief and continuity skills.
- The appendix supports practical audio capture, not comprehensive sound design, foley, ADR, music composition, mixing/mastering or current loudness standards.
- Modern color science (ACES, HDR, transforms, calibration), optical physics, digital cinema codecs, VFX/compositing, animation, distribution and delivery engineering are not comprehensively covered by this historical corpus.
- Model-specific prompting, current generative video constraints and licensing require separate up-to-date adapters. The core skills intentionally make no claims about named models.
- Behavioral testing is outstanding. Source attribution and format validation do not demonstrate an improvement in film quality.
