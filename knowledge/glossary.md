# Shared glossary

- **Beat:** a meaningful change in intention, action, knowledge or emotion; not necessarily a musical beat.
- **Scene:** a dramatic unit, often bounded by place/time. **Shot:** one continuous camera view or generated clip used in the edit. **Sequence:** multiple shots or scenes forming a larger unit.
- **Screen direction:** perceived direction in the image, distinct from physical world coordinates. **Action axis:** reference line between interacting subjects or along movement.
- **Coverage:** alternate views or portions needed to construct an edit. **Master:** a view containing the relevant action and spatial relationship. **Insert:** close detail within an established scene. **Cutaway:** related view outside the primary action.
- **POV:** distinguish a literal subjective view from a camera placed near a character's perspective.
- **Key / fill / separation:** dominant modeling illumination, control of shadow detail, and differentiation of subject from surroundings. These are functions rather than mandatory fixtures.
- **Motivation:** the dramatic, informational, physical or stylistic reason for a decision. A visible practical may motivate an offscreen supporting source.
- **Shot flow:** the movement of attention and meaning across adjacent frames, shots and edits.
- **Edit handles:** additional usable footage before or after the chosen interval.
- **World state:** canonical placement and conditions of actors, wardrobe, props, location, light and weather at a story-time boundary.
