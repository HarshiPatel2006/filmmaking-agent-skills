# Reading and coverage report

All five supplied files were inventoried locally: 1,027 PDF pages in total. The four text-bearing PDFs (781 pages) were extracted page-by-page and used for chapter mapping and selected passage review. The Mascelli PDF (246 pages) is image-only. Visual review used the chapter starts and representative pages of each chapter; the indexed preview exposed only the first 150 pages. Full manual close reading and scan transcription were not completed. This is a source-informed first release, not an exhaustive replacement for the books.

Read [source-coverage.csv](source-coverage.csv) for chapter-level dispositions and [source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json) for local file counts and hashes. `incorporated-selected` means relevant selected passages and chapter context informed the skills; `partial`/`limited` indicate narrower treatment; `visual-sampled` indicates scanned pages were inspected without full transcription. The page ranges are topic locators, not assertions of complete page coverage.

Selected visual review of Mascelli: PDF 1–41 available as indexed preview (camera-angle concepts and illustrations); local montage samples at PDF pages 60, 63, 64, 66–70, 80, 90, 100, 110, 120, 130, 140, 143–147, 155, 165, 168–173, 180, 190, 192–195, 200, 205, 210, 215, 220, 225, 230, 235, 240, 245. Smaller montage text may not be fully legible. The source locators refer to the local 246-page scan.
