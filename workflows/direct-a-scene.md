# Direct a scene

**Input:** Approved scene, location constraints, performance goals.

**Output:** Scene intent, blocking, coverage, camera and performance plan.

## Procedure

1. [Directorial interpretation](../skills/directorial-interpretation/SKILL.md) — Choose viewer relation and dramatic emphasis.
2. [Location geography](../skills/location-geography/SKILL.md) — Lock a common spatial map.
3. [Blocking and staging](../skills/blocking-and-staging/SKILL.md) — Stage changing relationships.
4. [Performance direction](../skills/performance-direction/SKILL.md) — Specify playable beats.
5. [Shot size and coverage](../skills/shot-size-and-coverage/SKILL.md) — Choose essential views.
6. [Screen direction](../skills/screen-direction/SKILL.md) — Check axis and eyelines.
7. [Coverage planning](../skills/coverage-planning/SKILL.md) — Ensure an editable plan.

## Approval gate

If a planned action cannot be seen, revise blocking or coverage before generating or shooting.

Record assumptions, versioned artifact IDs, approved state and unresolved risks at every handoff. The order is a default; iterate when a later test exposes an upstream problem.
