# Audio-led music video

**Input:** Approved audio master, observed/verified phrase map if available, creative constraints.

**Output:** Original story direction, variable shot plan, design requirements, video-ready specifications and edit plan.

## Procedure

1. [Premise and logline](../skills/premise-and-logline/SKILL.md) — Derive concepts from audio meaning; do not copy an existing video.
2. [Theme and motif](../skills/theme-and-motif/SKILL.md) — Define an emotional trajectory and visual motifs.
3. [Story architecture](../skills/story-architecture/SKILL.md) — Arrange narrative or lyrical arc.
4. [Editing rhythm](../skills/editing-rhythm/SKILL.md) — Use vocal, musical and emotional phrases; distinguish provisional timing.
5. [Production design brief](../skills/production-design-brief/SKILL.md) — Define characters, locations and reusable asset states.
6. [Storyboard design](../skills/storyboard-design/SKILL.md) — Plan each shot as a purposeful image/action.
7. [Animatic and shot flow](../skills/animatic-and-shot-flow/SKILL.md) — Test complete duration against audio.
8. [Coverage planning](../skills/coverage-planning/SKILL.md) — Plan generation/capture handles and alternatives.
9. [Creative quality audit](../skills/creative-quality-audit/SKILL.md) — Check music timing, story and continuity.

## Approval gate

Use audio as the master clock. Never invent timestamps or claim to have listened without audio access; request verified alignment or label provisional timing. A chosen capture/generation tool is an implementation constraint, not a core filmmaking rule. Do not add a new soundtrack without authorization.

Record assumptions, versioned artifact IDs, approved state and unresolved risks at every handoff. The order is a default; iterate when a later test exposes an upstream problem.
