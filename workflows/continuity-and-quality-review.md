# Continuity and quality review

**Input:** Ordered boards or footage and canonical production state.

**Output:** Prioritized defects, evidence, repairs and retest status.

## Procedure

1. [Screen direction](../skills/screen-direction/SKILL.md) — Audit axes and looks.
2. [Action continuity](../skills/action-continuity/SKILL.md) — Audit motion and state changes.
3. [Prop and costume continuity](../skills/prop-and-costume-continuity/SKILL.md) — Check stable assets.
4. [Lighting continuity](../skills/lighting-continuity/SKILL.md) — Check light world.
5. [Shot flow audit](../skills/shot-flow-audit/SKILL.md) — Evaluate editorial relationships.
6. [Creative quality audit](../skills/creative-quality-audit/SKILL.md) — Prioritize fixes by narrative impact.

## Approval gate

Do not claim rendered quality when only scripts or stills are available.

Record assumptions, versioned artifact IDs, approved state and unresolved risks at every handoff. The order is a default; iterate when a later test exposes an upstream problem.
