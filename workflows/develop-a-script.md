# Develop a script

**Input:** A creative seed, format, audience and constraints.

**Output:** A premise, causal outline, character records, screenplay and revision log.

## Procedure

1. [Premise and logline](../skills/premise-and-logline/SKILL.md) — Generate and compare distinct concepts.
2. [Theme and motif](../skills/theme-and-motif/SKILL.md) — Establish thematic tension and visual motifs.
3. [Conflict and stakes](../skills/conflict-and-stakes/SKILL.md) — Make the dramatic engine consequential.
4. [Character motivation](../skills/character-motivation/SKILL.md) — Lock goals and behavior.
5. [Story architecture](../skills/story-architecture/SKILL.md) — Arrange causal beats and viewer questions.
6. [Scene construction](../skills/scene-construction/SKILL.md) — Draft each scene around change.
7. [Dialogue and subtext](../skills/dialogue-and-subtext/SKILL.md) — Add or refine playable speech.
8. [Script revision](../skills/script-revision/SKILL.md) — Check purpose, progression and payoffs.

## Approval gate

No scene list is approved until major choices and consequences are understandable without explanatory commentary.

Record assumptions, versioned artifact IDs, approved state and unresolved risks at every handoff. The order is a default; iterate when a later test exposes an upstream problem.
