# Previsualization and coverage

Plan filmable, editable sequences before production.

- [Storyboard design](../skills/storyboard-design/SKILL.md) — ordered storyboard with framing, action, camera movement, audio and cut cues
- [Coverage planning](../skills/coverage-planning/SKILL.md) — shot list with priority, action ranges, camera setup and fallback
- [Camera angle projection](../skills/camera-angle-projection/SKILL.md) — camera placement diagram, viewpoint limits, assumptions and validation step
- [Animatic and shot flow](../skills/animatic-and-shot-flow/SKILL.md) — timed panel sequence, provisional edit decision list and issue log
