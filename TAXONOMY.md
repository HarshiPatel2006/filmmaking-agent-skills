# Filmmaking capability taxonomy

Categories represent the primary decision owned by a skill, not a department, source author or AI tool. Related skills can span categories.

| # | Category | Decision boundary | Skills |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 01 | Creative development | Define the concept, theme and audience experience. | 3 |
| 02 | Storytelling | Organize dramatic causality, stakes and audience knowledge. | 3 |
| 03 | Screenwriting | Construct, refine and communicate playable scenes. | 4 |
| 04 | Character development | Ground character behavior, relationships and performance. | 4 |
| 05 | Film directing | Translate dramatic intention into staging and visual decisions. | 5 |
| 06 | Cinematography | Choose camera viewpoints, shot scale, perspective and focus. | 5 |
| 07 | Composition and visual grammar | Control attention, depth, framing and format. | 4 |
| 08 | Camera movement | Design motivated moving shots and choreography. | 4 |
| 09 | Lighting and shadow | Shape tonal hierarchy, source logic, faces and continuity. | 6 |
| 10 | Atmosphere and special illumination | Design environmental and changing-source light. | 5 |
| 11 | Production design and visual world | Coordinate visual world, assets, geography and look. | 4 |
| 12 | Previsualization and coverage | Plan filmable, editable sequences before production. | 4 |
| 13 | Continuity | Maintain or intentionally alter spatial and physical state. | 2 |
| 14 | Editing and film rhythm | Construct meaningful relationships in screen time. | 4 |
| 15 | Practical production | Capture useful, reliable footage and sound. | 3 |
| 16 | Creative quality control | Diagnose defects and prioritize creative revisions. | 2 |

Foundational and specialized labels describe granularity, not measured proficiency. Composite workflows are separate orchestration guides; they do not duplicate canonical skills.
